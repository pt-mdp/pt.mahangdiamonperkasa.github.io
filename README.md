markdown# NovaPro - Complete Mosque & Islamic Center Management App (Flutter)

NovaPro is a premium, highly optimized, and full-featured Flutter application designed to digitize and simplify mosque (masjid) and prayer room (mushollah) administration. Built with a modern UI/UX, this app works 100% OFFLINE, ensuring high data privacy, instant speed, and zero monthly server maintenance costs for mosque committees.

## 🌟 Key Features & Capabilities
- **Multi-Language Support:** Fully localized and ready-to-use in English, Arabic (العربية), and Bahasa Indonesia.
- **Worship Tools:** Built-in offline Surah Yasin (83 complete verses) and Juz Amma (37 complete chapters) with Arabic, Latin, English/Indonesian translations, and auto-save bookmarking.
- **Prayer Schedules:** Highly accurate prayer times based on location with automatic adhan notifications.
- **Live Display Mode:** Seamlessly responsive for TV/projector screens with automated slides, announcements, and running text.
- **Financial Cash Flow Management:** Easily track donors, cash flows, and generate instant PDF reports. Supports 25+ global currencies (USD, SAR, MYR, IDR, SGD, EUR, and more).
- **Inventory & Asset Control:** Keep structural track of mosque properties and asset values.
- **Friday Khutbah Schedule:** Manage administrator data, upcoming preachers, imams, and weekly sermon themes with photos and contact details.
